# Phase-Controlled-Coaxial-Rotors
Toolsets to parametrically model the aerodynamics, controls, and performance for independently controlled coaxial rotor systems for vertical lift aircraft.

## Overview
Models were developed and validated through the PhD work of Matthew Asper at the University of Texas at Austin from 2019-2024.

## Reference Documentation
1. Asper, M., "Active Phasing for Thrust Control of Electric Rotorcaft," _The University of Texas at Austin,_ 2024, doi: [10.26153/tsw/58133](https://doi.org/10.26153/tsw/58133).
2. Asper, M., Sirohi, J., and Ricci, M., “Analysis and Testing of Index Angle-Controlled Stacked Rotors in Hover,” _Journal
of the American Helicopter Society,_ Vol. 70(3), 2025, pp. 1-12, doi: [10.4050/JAHS.70.032006](https://doi.org/10.4050/JAHS.70.032006).
